Two rescue paths.
Neither transfers.

Late feature death in the H=16D, K=4D fuzzy MLP is real, but neither bias-only homeostasis nor a zero-forward ghost gradient is a robust remedy. The first redistributes competition without preserving whole groups at scale; the second barely moves survival and worsens validation.

Rejected path 1

Balance / homeostasis

A small activation-frequency floor nearly halves persistent death at 60M and lowers it 43% at 125M. At 250M the reduction shrinks to 14%, dead groups increase, and validation worsens by 0.0113 nats. More even winners are not enough when every member of a group falls below ReLU.

Rejected path 2

Ghost-AuxK surrogate

Tied initialization plus a dead-feature zero-forward cotangent improves survival only 0.29–0.57 percentage points over tied initialization, while adding 11–16% train time and worsening validation. It never advances beyond the 60M mechanism screen.

What was actually tested

These negative results are intentionally narrow. They should not be read as a rejection of reconstruction-based AuxK.

Balance path. The selected straight-through frequency floor measures positive winners over the global batch, targets 2.5% per feature inside each four-way group, and sends auxiliary gradient only to deficient entries of the existing encoder bias. It adds no model parameter or evaluation operation.

Development evidence. Group-survival hinges reduced death by making activations much denser; hard-load objectives balanced winners but barely changed death; a smooth Switch-style objective cost 51% more wall time. All remain archived as the selection trail.

Ghost path. A feature becomes eligible after 10M inactive tokens. Eight rotating cohorts select positive dead choices, then inject g − stop_gradient(g). The numerical forward is zero; selected encoder columns and decoder rows receive the downstream language-model cotangent.

Missing ingredient. Ghost-AuxK has neither a reconstruction decoder nor a stopped reconstruction residual. The later train-only reconstruction-decoder experiment supplies that missing target and is a separate research line.
